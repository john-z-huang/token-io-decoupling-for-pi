应用[Mode](../references/coding/layer-02-workflow-concepts/entry-and-context/mode.yaml)、[模式确认](../references/coding/layer-01-fundamental-concepts/delegation/delegation-mode-confirmation.yaml)、[委派数量门禁](../references/coding/layer-01-fundamental-concepts/delegation/delegation-mode-count-gate.yaml)、[委派状态记录](../references/coding/layer-01-fundamental-concepts/delegation/delegation-state-record.yaml)和[模式重入](../references/coding/layer-01-fundamental-concepts/delegation/delegation-mode-reentry.yaml)。核验准确的已释放模式及锁定数量/状态；Single-Agent 要求 `child_count: 0` 且无 allocations。Multi-Agent 要求正数锁定数量及有效且与记录一致的当前 allocation；已占用/已创建和未分配的名额都可能是有效状态，不得要求每个名额都保持未绑定。已释放证据缺失或过期时返回 `needs_prerequisite`；模式/count 与已选路线冲突时返回 `blocked`。后续指令复用已锁定拓扑。这只确认路线资格，不释放具体 Slice 或授权创建子代理。以当前门禁证据返回 `complete`。
